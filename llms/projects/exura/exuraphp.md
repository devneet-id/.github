# exuraphp

**Repo:** `devneet-id/exuraphp` · **Language:** PHP

> A minimalist PHP micro-framework for simplifying modern backend development.

## Role in the series

The **backend** layer of the Exura series. A small, focused micro-framework for building modern backends and REST APIs without the weight of a full-stack framework.

## Philosophy

- Minimalist and semantic-first (shared across all Exura packages).
- Lightweight; aims to do less so developers can build more.
- Usable independently of exurajs/exuracss and of Rune.

## For an AI

- Distributed for PHP projects (Composer). Confirm the exact package name/version on Packagist before depending on it.
- Keep APIs consistent in spirit with exurajs (frontend) so the series feels like one family.
