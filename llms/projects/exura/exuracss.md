# exuracss

**Repo:** `devneet-id/exuracss` · **Language:** CSS

> A modern, semantic-first CSS framework.

## Role in the series

The **styling** layer of the Exura series. Semantic-first: style follows meaning (semantic HTML) rather than a wall of utility classes.

## Philosophy

- Semantic-first and minimalist (the defining trait of this package).
- Modern CSS; lightweight.
- Usable independently of exuraphp/exurajs.
